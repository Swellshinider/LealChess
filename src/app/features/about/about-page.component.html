Open-source chess training

About

LealChess is a local-first chess training desk for playing, studying your own games, and practicing the decisions that cost you the most.

How it helps

Train on the games that matter

Play

Play complete games against Stockfish and keep your active game on this device.

Study

Import Chess.com and Lichess games, then review them with local analysis.

Improve

Turn mistakes and missed opportunities into focused practice positions.

Local by design

Your chess work stays yours

LealChess has no account or cloud database. Games, preferences, and analysis stay in this browser. It contacts Chess.com or Lichess only when you ask to import games.
