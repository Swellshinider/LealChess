About LealChess

LealChess is a local-first chess training desk that turns the positions you actually played into focused practice.

  1. 01 Play Finish a game
  2. 02 Review Find the turning points
  3. 03 Practice Replay the decisions
  4. 04 Improve Bring the lesson back

Why LealChess

Most chess tools separate play, analysis, and practice. LealChess keeps them at one desk, built around the games you actually played.

Our Mission

Help players spot the decisions that changed a game, understand better choices, and return to those positions until the lesson sticks.

Your games, preferences, and analysis stay in this browser. There is no LealChess account or cloud database.

Opening Puzzles contacts Lichess and Chess.com for their daily puzzles. Puzzle progress remains on this device and is never submitted to either provider.

We Are Open Source

Anyone can inspect how LealChess works, follow its direction, and help make local chess study better.
